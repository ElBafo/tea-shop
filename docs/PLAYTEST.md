# First-session playtest

Status: prepared for the preview; human observation has not happened yet. Automated checks establish that the paths work, not that a new player understands them or wants to return.

Use the [regular preview](https://elbafo.github.io/tea-shop/preview/) in Safari. Record the version on Start, phone model, browser or Home Screen shortcut, and whether the player has seen Decopu before. Leave sound to the player's preference.

For a truly fresh session, use a device/profile with no preview progress. Alternatively, the preview's More menu has **Reset preview progress**, with confirmation. That action clears the current device's preview save; use it only when the owner wants to discard that save. Main progress is separate. The temporary tour is useful for checking guidance but is not a natural saved first session.

Tell the player only: “Try this game and make the little shop yours.” Do not explain the jars, point at buttons, recommend a decoration or tell them which move to make. Let them choose the tour or explore. Stop or help if they ask; record where help was needed. There is no time limit to beat.

Observe this short sequence:

| Moment | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Start and welcome | Do they understand the puzzle → stars → room connection? Which welcome option do they choose? |
| First transfer | Drag or tap? Any missed targets, reversed gestures, hesitation or need for coaching? |
| First solve | Do they expect full jars? Is the last move and earned reward understandable? |
| First decoration | Can they choose a style before buying, understand the price, cancel a preview and return to browsing? |
| Arrangement | Can they move the purchased item immediately, finish editing and find the next puzzle? |
| Natural stopping point | Do they choose another puzzle or identify a decoration they want? Record the choice, without prompting continued play. |

After the first session, ask: “What felt confusing?”, “What did you enjoy most?” and “What would you want to do next?” Keep their words separate from our interpretation.

Review later mechanics separately, once naturally unlocked: puzzle 5 introduces hidden contents, puzzle 8 uses matching pairs, and puzzle 18 has five-slot jars. Do not unlock boards or award stars in the player's save for testing. Note whether the new mechanic is understood and whether rejected moves explain the problem. Solvability checks alone do not establish enjoyable difficulty.

Record findings in this form:

```
Version / device / launch mode:
New or returning player / guided or skipped:
Observed action or exact comment:
Screen and puzzle number:
Help provided (if any):
Impact: blocked / repeated confusion / minor hesitation / preference
Hypothesis (separate from observation):
Proposed change and how to check it:
```

Prioritize blocked progress, misunderstood rules and lost purchases before decorative preferences. Change authored boards or star targets only after reviewing those observations and rechecking solvability, rewards and save compatibility. Record decisions in ROADMAP.md. One positive session is useful feedback, not evidence of long-term retention.
