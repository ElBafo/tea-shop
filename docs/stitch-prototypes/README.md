## Initial inspection — separate from originals

- Visually inspected: Living Diorama portrait, Babylon.js-labelled sorting stage, Tea Book Compendium, Guest Friendship Story Log, and the standalone traditional Japanese tea-house night illustration. Other captures are inventoried, not individually signed off.
- The Living Diorama capture contains a missing central room image/visible alt text. Preserve that capture as evidence; use the standalone room illustrations as separate art references rather than silently editing the original.
- The Babylon.js-labelled sorting stage depicts a raster workshop image with UI controls. Text inspection of all 25 HTML exports finds no `<canvas>` or Babylon/Three scene initialization; no editable 3D model files are included. Engine labels, camera controls and pouring imagery are design references, not proof of a playable 3D implementation.
- The HTML sources depend on Tailwind CDN, Google Fonts/icons and externally hosted Google image URLs. They are not self-contained offline prototypes. No external assets were downloaded, no HTML was executed, and these dependencies were not installed into the game.
- Three HTML exports have no paired screenshot: **3D tea sort puzzle**, **Starry Midnight Living Diorama**, and **Tea Sorting Mechanics Step-by-Step Guide**. No screenshot is fabricated for them.
- The inspected images support the honey/matcha palette, soft card/button treatment, botanical room art and separate recipe/scrapbook surfaces. They also contain proposed currencies, rewards and game controls that remain subject to the existing [v1 puzzle-flow reconciliation](../V1_PUZZLE_FLOW.md).
- This import does not approve orbit controls, jar locks, hint/extra-jar limits, new rewards/currencies, or simultaneous hub/puzzle layout as changes to released gameplay. Nor does it demonstrate physical-phone fit/performance or editable 3D assets.
